    nerd-herd Spaces mod: groups of machines

    nerd-herd is a set of Herdr mods, and its full scope is not settled yet. This spec covers one of them: a Spaces mod that replaces the sidebar’s Spaces section and gathers machines into groups (for example Personal and Work). The Agents section has its own spec.

    Stage
    completed
    Complexity
    6 / 10 for the first build — one mod with nested groups, drag and drop, per-group sorts, settings, downloaded banners with error cards, and a test harness for a native app not yet proven
    Toolchain
    TypeScript on Bun. bun build compiles each mod into one ES module with herdr, gpui-kit, and gpui-base left as imports; a probe built this way passed herdr-app check on 2026-11-16.
    Herdr version
    0.1.16 (app), read from /Applications/Herdr.app/Contents/Info.plist
    Standing rule
    Where Herdr already provides a feature, use it, unless the idea does not fit it (from the user, 2026-11-16).
    Design reviewer Implementation lead Test reviewer

    Narrative

    Dannel renames the sidebar's word for groups to "Environments", makes a Work environment with an emoji icon and a banner link, nests a Clients environment inside it, drags desk-work and This computer into Work, sorts and folds the environments, and after Herdr restarts sees those machines' spaces still inside the folded Work environment in their custom order.

    Organizing machines into groups

    Claims
    1. The new-group button in Spaces adds an empty group that Dannel names
    2. Dropping a machine on a group lists that machine's spaces inside the group
    3. Dropping a machine between two machines puts it at that position
    4. Choosing alphabetical or reverse alphabetical sorts machines by name, and choosing custom again restores the dragged order
    5. Folding a group hides its machines and keeps its header with the group's machine count: on its row beside its name, or on its banner
    6. A group without a loaded banner shows its chosen icon and name in its row
    7. Groups, their machines, the order, the fold state, and the look are the same after Herdr restarts
    8. An unreachable machine stays in its group and says it is unreachable
    9. This computer can be dropped into a group like any machine
    10. Each group sorts what it holds, custom by default, with alphabetical and reverse alphabetical as the other choices
    11. Deleting a group moves its machines to the ungrouped machines below the groups
    12. Ungrouped machines show below the groups with no header, or under a header when that setting is on
    13. Changing the word for groups to Environments shows Environments wherever the sidebar names groups
    14. The cog wheel beside the Spaces + opens Spaces settings
    15. Dropping a group on another group nests it inside that group
    16. Dropping a group into one of its own subgroups leaves every group where it was
    17. The Ungrouped header is renamed the same way as any group
    18. A banner link is downloaded and shown in place of the group's row
    19. A banner carries the group's controls on top of it: the cog, the + for a new subgroup, the machine count while folded, and the fold chevron
    20. Each group can hide the fold chevron on its banner, and it shows by default
    21. Each group places the controls on its banner at the top, middle, or bottom
    22. A group whose banner is still loading shows its plain row until the banner is ready
    23. A group with one banner link shows that banner whether it is folded or open
    24. A group with an open banner and a folded banner shows the one that matches its state
    25. A banner shows at its image's own pixel size, and the sidebar edge clips whatever is wider
    26. A wide banner is clipped on the side its group chose, keeping the left edge by default
    27. A banner link that fails to load shows the group's plain row with an error card beneath it saying why
    28. Renaming a group changes its name and keeps everything inside it
    29. Dropping a group at the top level or between two groups moves it there
    30. Dropping a machine below the groups takes it out of its group
    31. A banner link that does not start with https is refused with a message saying why
    32. Everything Herdr’s own Spaces section does still works for every machine, inside groups or not
    33. Right-clicking a group header opens its menu, and its cog opens its settings
    34. Deleting a group moves its subgroups up to its place
    35. Rows can be moved, folded, and renamed from the keyboard
    36. Cutting a row and pasting it onto a group moves the row into that group
    37. A banner download larger than 2 MB shows the error card
    38. Inside a group, its subgroups come before its machines, the subgroups ordered by its subgroup sort and the machines by its machine sort
    39. A group name that is empty or longer than 60 characters is refused with a message saying why
    40. Each group's folded count shows every machine inside by default, or only direct machines, or all direct items
    41. Damaged saved groups are kept as a backup and Spaces shows Herdr's usual list with an error card

    Dannel works on a personal machine and on machines for two contexts: their own projects and their job at Job. The Spaces section lists five machines in name order, so personal and work machines are mixed together. They click the new-group button beside the Spaces "+" button, and a group appears with its name ready to edit. They type "Work" and pick an icon for it.

    They drag desk-work from the list and drop it on the Work group. The machine and its spaces now sit inside Work. They make a Personal group the same way, drop desk-home and ai-sandbox into it, and drag ai-sandbox above desk-home. Later they switch the sort to alphabetical to find a machine by name. Then they switch back to custom, and their dragged order returns.

    When they focus on personal work, they fold Work. Its header stays, showing "Work" and how many machines it holds. laptop-travel is switched off today, and it stays in Personal marked as unreachable. The next morning Herdr restarts after an update, and the sidebar shows the same groups, order, folds, and icons as the evening before.

    "Groups" is not quite how Dannel thinks of these, so they change the sidebar's word for them to "Environments". The new-group button, the headers, and the menus now say "environment" instead. They drag This computer into Personal too, because it is a personal machine like the others. They try a temporary "Scratch" environment, holding ai-sandbox, then delete it; ai-sandbox moves down to the ungrouped machines below the environments. Last, they set the environments to sort alphabetically, so Personal shows above Work, while the machines inside each one keep their own sort.

    They made that word change in Spaces settings, which the cog wheel beside the Spaces "+" opens. In the same place they turn on the ungrouped header, and the ungrouped machines gather under "Ungrouped". They rename that header "Unsorted" the same way they rename any group. Work grows a "Clients" environment, which they drag onto Work so it nests inside it, with Work's own sort deciding where it sits. When they accidentally drag Work onto Clients, nothing moves. Finally they paste a banner link for Work, and after a moment a wide image runs across Work's header. The link they first tried for Clients was mistyped, and Clients' header showed a card in the banner's place: the link returned 404 Not Found, and they should check it in Spaces settings.

    Questions

    Spec or build order

    Question
    Is this design worked out in a spec, or built straight from the request?
    Resolution
    A spec in docs/specs/, because the design still needs back and forth.

    Skeleton sentence

    Question
    Does the narrative's skeleton journey say what "it works" means for the first build?
    Resolution
    Yes, as written in the Narrative.

    Damaged saved data

    Question
    What does Spaces do when the saved groups or settings it reads fail the shape check?
    Resolution
    Keep the unreadable data aside under a backup key and start with no groups. Spaces then looks like Herdr without the mod (this computer first, then the other machines by name), except for an error card saying the saved groups could not be read and were kept as a backup. The card keeps one reference id for as long as the backup is kept, and has two buttons (revised 2026-11-17 by the user): Try again reads the backup again and restores it when it can be read now, and Dismiss hides the card and keeps the backup. A dismissal lasts across restarts; damaged data found after it shows the card again.

    Group name rules

    Question
    Which group names are accepted?
    Resolution
    Not empty after trimming spaces and at most 60 characters. Duplicate names are allowed, because groups are told apart by an internal id, never by name (the user, 2026-11-16).

    Folded group count

    Question
    With nesting, what number does a folded group's header show?
    Resolution
    A setting on each group chooses it: every machine inside at any depth (the default), only machines directly inside, or everything directly inside (groups and machines together).

    How machines are put in groups

    Question
    How does a machine get assigned to a group?
    Resolution
    All in the sidebar: a new-group button beside the Spaces "+", drag and drop to move and reorder machines, a sort choice of custom, alphabetical, or reverse alphabetical, collapsible groups, an icon and a banner per group, all kept by Herdr so it survives restarts.

    Group icon source

    Question
    Where does a group's icon come from?
    Resolution
    An emoji for now. The saved icon records its kind (emoji today), so icon sets such as the Noun Project can be added later as another kind without changing saved emoji icons.

    Group nomenclature

    Question
    What does the sidebar call a group?
    Resolution
    "Groups" by default, changeable by the user (for example "Environments" or "Projects"). The code always says "group"; every word shown to the user comes from settings data.

    Spaces settings

    Question
    Where does the user change the word for groups and the other Spaces settings?
    Resolution
    A cog wheel next to the Spaces "+" button opens the Spaces settings, and they are changed there.

    Group word forms

    Question
    Does the group-word setting hold a separate singular and plural form?
    Resolution
    Yes: separate singular and plural fields in Spaces settings, because English plurals are irregular ("Property", "Properties").

    Ungrouped header wording

    Question
    When the ungrouped-header setting is on, what does that header say?
    Resolution
    "Ungrouped" by default, and the user renames it the same way as any other group.

    Group banner source

    Question
    Where does a group's banner come from?
    Resolution
    A web link (URL). A group has no banner, one banner, or two: an open banner and a folded banner. When only one is set, it shows in both states. Each banner shows at its full banner height in its original proportions, and the sidebar clips the part wider than the sidebar.
    Context
    Herdr's image(path) loads only a file inside Herdr's app folder (~/.config/herdr-app), per its doc comment in gpui-kit.d.ts. So Banner cache downloads the link and saves it as a file the sidebar can show. How the download works is Banner research.

    Banner height

    Question
    How tall is a banner drawn in the sidebar?
    Resolution
    The image's own pixel height, however tall. The user sizes the image to suit.

    Banner clip side

    Question
    When a banner is wider than the sidebar, which part stays visible?
    Resolution
    A choice on each group: the left edge (the default, clipping the right side) or the center (clipping both sides equally).

    Where group options are set

    Question
    Where does the user set a group’s own options: icon, banner links, clip side, subgroup and machine sorts, and folded count?
    Resolution
    Both: right-clicking a group header opens a menu (Rename, Delete, Move to…, Settings), and a cog button on the group header, the same cog icon Spaces uses for its own settings, opens that group’s settings: icon, banner links, clip side, subgroup and machine sorts, and folded count.

    Deleting a group with subgroups

    Question
    When a deleted group holds other groups, what happens to them?
    Resolution
    Its subgroups move up to the deleted group’s place, keeping their contents. Its machines move to the ungrouped machines.

    Keyboard use

    Question
    Must groups work from the keyboard as well as the mouse?
    Resolution
    Yes. Arrow keys move between rows, Enter folds and unfolds, F2 renames, the group menu’s “Move to…” moves a row, and cut and paste move a row: Cmd+X and Cmd+V on macOS, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V on Windows and Linux. The keys are bindings in the mod:nerd-herd-spaces section of Herdr’s keys.json, so the user can rebind them and they apply only while the Spaces mod has the keyboard (Herdr’s guide: a mod:<name> section applies while that mod has focus), leaving Ctrl+X to terminal programs elsewhere. The Spaces mod opts in to the keyboard with "focusable": true in its mod.json.

    Banner size limit

    Question
    What is the largest banner file nerd-herd downloads?
    Resolution
    2 MB. A larger download shows the error card.

    Banner research

    Question
    Can a Herdr mod download an image to a file, and draw it at an exact size clipped on the left or in the center?
    Resolution
    Yes, with curl through Herdr’s exec for the download, not Herdr’s fetch.
    Evidence
    2026-11-16, a probe mod in the test window (.e2e-home/): Herdr’s fetch answered status 200 but its response had no arrayBuffer() (TypeError: not a function). fs.writeFile saved a 3-byte Uint8Array and read it back. curl printed 200 image/png 13504, and the saved file began with the PNG signature 137,80,78,71. curl --proto =https refused an http link with Protocol "http" disabled. A screenshot showed a 544 by 184 image drawn at that size in a 268-wide overflow_x_hidden box with its right side clipped, and a second box with the image shifted by a negative left margin (ml(-138)) showing its center.

    Banner as the group's row

    Question
    Does a banner sit beneath the group's row, or take its place?
    Resolution
    It takes the row's place: a group shows its row or its banner, never both. The icon and name are hidden while the banner shows. The group's controls sit on top of the banner: the cog (group settings), the + (a new subgroup inside this group), the machine count while folded, and the fold chevron. Each group can hide the chevron, for example when its open and folded banners already show the state; it shows by default. Each group places the controls at the top, middle, or bottom of the banner. While the banner is loading, and when it fails, the plain row shows instead, with the error card beneath it on a failure.
    Context
    The user, 2026-11-17: "when the banner is present, it would not show the row we display … the banner would effectively become that row, but you would still put any icons on top of it". The default placement was not stated; middle is in use until the user names one.

    Banner that fails to load

    Question
    What does a group's header show when its banner link cannot be downloaded or is not an image?
    Resolution
    The group's plain row (icon, name, controls), with an error card beneath it giving a clear message saying why, such as "404 Not Found" (revised 2026-11-17, with Banner as the group's row). Each failure is its own error type carrying its facts as fields, and the card's message is rendered from those fields with the next step, per the user's error-handling standard.

    Group nesting

    Question
    Can a group hold other groups?
    Resolution
    Yes, groups inside groups.

    Sort scope

    Question
    Does one sort choice apply everywhere, or does each group get its own?
    Resolution
    Each group gets its own sorts for what it holds, and the top level of Spaces has them too: a subgroup sort and a machine sort, both custom by default, that the user can set differently.

    Groups and machines side by side

    Question
    Inside a group that holds both groups and machines, how are the two kinds ordered?
    Resolution
    Subgroups first, then machines, like folders before files in a file browser. Changed 2026-11-17 by the user: this is each level's default, "Groups first". Dropping one kind among the other switches that level to a custom order, and a level's settings switch it back. The subgroups follow the group's subgroup sort and the machines its machine sort.

    Linked sorts

    Question
    While a group's subgroup sort and machine sort are still the same, does changing the subgroup sort change the machine sort too?
    Resolution
    No. The two sorts are independent; they only happen to start the same (both custom). Each changes only when the user sets that sort.

    One sorting concept

    Question
    Are the group sort and the machine sort two features, or one?
    Resolution
    One. A single sort order (custom, alphabetical, reverse alphabetical) works on any list of named items, so groups and machines use it now and other nerd-herd mods, such as the Agents mod, can use it later.

    Group order under sorting

    Question
    When the sort is alphabetical, are the groups themselves also sorted by name, or only the machines inside them?
    Resolution
    Groups have their own sort, separate from the machines' sort: custom (the order the user arranges) by default, alphabetical, or reverse alphabetical.

    Machines outside any group

    Question
    Where does a machine that is in no group show, including a machine added later?
    Resolution
    Below the groups with no header by default. A setting puts them under a header instead.

    Deleting a group

    Question
    What happens to a group's machines when the group is deleted?
    Resolution
    They move to the ungrouped machines below the groups.

    This computer in groups

    Question
    Where does "This computer" go now that groups exist?
    Resolution
    It is treated like any other machine: the user groups it however they like.

    Header size

    Question
    Are bigger, higher-contrast section, group, and machine headers part of the first build?
    Resolution
    No. Headers keep Herdr's current size (11 to 11.5 pixel text) for now.

    Group storage scope

    Question
    Is it enough that groups are kept on this computer only?
    Resolution
    Yes. Herdr's built-in shared mod state, saved to ~/.config/herdr-app/shared.json on this computer.

    End-to-end test harness

    Question
    Can a second, isolated Herdr window run beside the real one so the whole mod is tested without touching the real window or its settings?
    Resolution
    Partly. A second window started with HERDR_APP_HOME set to .e2e-home/ in this repository runs beside the real one with its own settings, mods, layouts, and saved mod state (shared.json), and answers herdr-app status, ls, and shot on its own. It shares the real herdr-core, so it shows the real spaces, machines, and live terminals. Tests may therefore change nerd-herd’s saved groups and settings freely, and may select spaces (focus is per window), but must run no command that changes spaces, tabs, or terminals.
    Evidence
    2026-11-16: the test window’s herdr-app ls listed the same space ids as the real window’s (for example sp9d62280b490bfa27ed8988c2a952e5e0, a-project), while its status showed its own zoom (1.2) and its own welcome overlay. A first try from the session scratch folder failed with path must be shorter than SUN_LEN: the window’s control socket path must stay under the macOS limit, so the test home has to be a short path.
    Banner downloads in tests
    The test window is started with a fake curl first on its PATH. That fake copies e2e/fixtures/work-banner.png (240 by 60, text “WORK BANNER” in its left 150 pixels so the controls drawn on a banner’s right do not cover it; made with magick -size 240x60 xc:'#cccccc' -fill '#555555' -font "/System/Library/Fonts/Supplemental/Arial Bold.ttf" -pointsize 17 -gravity west -annotate +12+0 'WORK BANNER' -strip, replacing the 2026-11-16 placehold.co image whose centered text the controls covered) and prints the status, content type, and size the real call would print. Tests then need no internet, and the production code is unchanged. Evidence, 2026-11-16: in a test window started with a fake curl first on its PATH, Herdr’s exec("curl", ["--probe"]) printed FAKE-CURL --probe.
    Driving the mod
    Tests act through Herdr commands to the mod, never the mouse or keyboard: herdr-app do nerd-herd-spaces.<action>:<JSON>. The mod’s nextCommand() receives the text after the mod name, so the mod reads an action name and a JSON payload, and checks the payload against an ArkType schema like any outside input. Evidence, 2026-11-16: a probe mod received herdr-app do cmd-probe.make:Hello World 💼 as make:Hello World 💼, while a --name argument was dropped.
    Reading the screen
    Tests read what the sidebar draws from herdr-app shot screenshots with Apple’s Vision text recognition (e2e/support/read-text.swift, compiled to .e2e-bin/read-text). Evidence, 2026-11-16: it read WORK BANNER from the fixture in 0.12 seconds after a one-time 25-second model load.

    Process Flow

    Module Definitions

    Group model

    Responsibility
    Turns saved groups, the group sort, the machine sort, and Herdr's machine list (this computer included) into the ordered rows Spaces shows, and applies a group action to saved groups.
    Owns
    Group membership, custom order of groups and of machines, sort rules, fold state, where ungrouped machines go.
    Delegates
    Ordering to Sort order. Otherwise data in, data out. It never holds a display word: rows name groups by id, and Spaces sidebar mod picks the words.
    Input SavedGroups, Machine[], GroupAction
    The saved groups, Herdr's machines, and one action (make, rename, delete, move a machine, nest a group, reorder, fold, set a sort, set icon, set open banner link, set folded banner link).
    Output SavedGroups, SpacesRow[]
    The new saved groups, and the rows to draw.

    Saved group store

    Responsibility
    Reads and writes saved groups through Herdr's shared state.
    Owns
    The shared-state key and the check that what was read has the expected shape.
    Delegates
    Saving to Herdr (shared and setShared).
    Input unknown
    Whatever is stored under the key.
    Output SavedGroups
    Checked saved groups.

    Sort order

    Responsibility
    Orders any list of named items by one sort choice: custom (the order given), alphabetical, or reverse alphabetical.
    Owns
    The sort choices and how each one orders items.
    Delegates
    Nothing. It knows names and order, not groups or machines.
    Input SortChoice, Named[]
    A sort choice and items, each with a name, in their custom order.
    Output Named[]
    The same items in the chosen order. The custom order itself is never changed.

    Banner cache

    Responsibility
    Turns a group's banner link into a file inside Herdr's app folder that Herdr's image() can show.
    Owns
    Where downloaded banners are kept, and which link each file came from. A saved file is named from a hash of its bytes, never from the link, so no part of a pasted link reaches a file path (the user’s security standard). A banner may also name a file on this computer (/…, ~/…, or file://…; the user, 2026-11-17: "What if I have the banner on disk and it's not hosted anywhere?"). That file is read through Herdr's file access, which reaches only the home folder, then checked the same way (2 MB at most, a PNG, GIF, or JPEG header) and kept as a copy named from its hash. Reading a local file is not communication, so the https rule below does not cover it. Only https links are downloaded (the same standard: HTTPS for all communication), enforced twice: the link is checked before download, and curl runs with --proto =https. curl also runs with --max-filesize 2097152 (2 MB) and reports the status, content type, and size, which are checked against an ArkType schema before the file is kept.
    Delegates
    Downloading to an injected downloader; reading and writing files to an injected file store. In the real mod the downloader runs curl through Herdr's exec, and the file store is Herdr's fs/promises. Herdr's fetch cannot be used: its response offers only text() and json(), with no binary body and no headers (ShellFetchResponse in gpui-kit.d.ts).
    Input BannerLink
    The link the user pasted.
    Output BannerFile
    The path of the saved image, relative to Herdr's app folder, and the image's width and height in pixels. Herdr's elements offer no "fit" option for images (no object_fit in gpui-kit.d.ts), so Spaces sidebar mod sizes the image from these numbers and clips it with a container's overflow_x_hidden.
    Throws BannerError
    One error type per failure, each under the package's root error type with its facts as fields (link, host, HTTP status and reason, content type) and a userAction: an HTTP 4xx answer or a non-image is fix (correct the link); an unreachable host or an HTTP 5xx answer is retry and its message carries the error's reference id.

    Sidebar settings

    Responsibility
    The one typed, validated place for user-changeable sidebar settings, each with one default declared beside it.
    Owns
    The word for groups (default "Group" and "Groups"), whether ungrouped machines get a header (default off), that header's name (default "Ungrouped"), and the settings' shared-state key. The cog wheel beside the Spaces "+" is where the user changes them.
    Delegates
    Saving to Herdr (shared and setShared).
    Input unknown
    Whatever is stored under the settings key.
    Output SidebarSettings
    Checked settings with every default applied.

    Spaces sidebar mod

    Responsibility
    Draws the Spaces rows and turns clicks and drops into group actions.
    Owns
    Layout, drag and drop, the new-group button.
    Delegates
    Rules to Group model; saving to Saved group store; display words to Sidebar settings; space commands to Herdr.

    Validation Boundaries
    • Saved groups read from Herdr's shared state are external data: another mod, a hand edit, or an older nerd-herd version may have written them. Saved group store checks them on every read against a Zod schema, which is also the single source of their TypeScript types. The user’s data standard names ArkType; ArkType failed inside Herdr’s mod runtime (no FormData, then “'valueOf' is read-only” under its frozen built-ins, both seen in herdr-app check on 2026-11-16), and Zod in its jitless mode loads there, so Zod stands in for it in this mod. Each group has a ULID as its internal id. Data that fails the check is kept under a backup key, Spaces starts with no groups, and an error card says so (Damaged saved data).
    • Herdr's machines, spaces, and agents come typed from herdr.d.ts. A group may name a machine Herdr no longer lists, so Group model treats a missing machine as data, not an error.
    • Group names and icons typed by the user are checked when entered: a name is not empty after trimming spaces, and is at most 60 characters; two groups may share a name (Group name rules). An icon is saved with its kind, and the only kind accepted today is emoji.
    • Sidebar settings read from Herdr's shared state are external data too. Sidebar settings checks them on every read and fills each missing value from its one declared default.

    Ambiguity Resolution

    "Stored with Herdr" means Herdr's shared mod state, which Herdr saves to ~/.config/herdr-app/shared.json on this computer and loads at start (Group storage scope).

    "Machine" means any machine Herdr lists, this computer included. The code never says "remote": most machines are remote, but this computer is a machine like the rest, and the user groups this computer like any other (This computer in groups). Herdr leaves out temporary machines, and so does nerd-herd.

    "Group" is the code's word. The word the user sees comes from Sidebar settings, "Groups" by default, so a test that checks displayed text supplies its own settings rather than relying on the default.

    Herdr’s own Spaces section already does a lot that users rely on (selecting spaces, adding a space on a machine, machine status, reordering spaces within a machine, nesting worktree spaces, the narrow rail). nerd-herd’s Spaces mod starts from a copy of that mod made with mod.clone, as Herdr’s guide directs, so those features keep working inside groups.

    The Spaces mod runs a program (curl) through Herdr’s exec. Herdr’s guide asks that anyone adding a shared mod be told when it runs programs, so a published nerd-herd says so.

    The shipped spaces mod belongs to Herdr. Herdr's guide says to copy them with mod.clone before changing them, and to copy a layout with layout.clone, so nerd-herd's mods go into a copied layout and the user's main layout stays as it is.

    Test Cases

    Error scenarios

    Nesting a group inside its own subgroup changes nothing

    Given
    Work holds the group Clients
    When
    The nest action puts Work inside Clients
    Then
    The saved groups equal the saved groups before the action: Work at the top level, holding Clients

    An empty group name is refused

    Given
    No groups
    When
    The make-group action runs with the name " " (three spaces)
    Then
    It throws a group-name error with userAction fix and reason "empty", and the saved groups are unchanged

    A 61-character group name is refused

    Given
    No groups
    When
    The make-group action runs with a 61-character name
    Then
    It throws a group-name error with userAction fix, reason "too long", limit 60, and length 61, and the saved groups are unchanged

    A refused name explains itself in the chosen group word

    Given
    The group word is Environment and Environments
    When
    Dannel names a new environment with a 61-character name
    Then
    The name field shows "Environment names can be at most 60 characters. This one has 61."

    Unreadable saved groups are backed up and reset

    Given
    The saved-groups key holds the text "not groups"
    When
    The store reads saved groups
    Then
    It returns no groups, the backup key holds "not groups", and it reports a saved-groups error with userAction report

    With no groups, Spaces lists machines the way Herdr does

    Given
    No groups, and Herdr lists desk-work, this computer, and ai-sandbox
    When
    Spaces rows are computed
    Then
    The machine rows are this computer, ai-sandbox, desk-work, with no header rows

    An http banner link is refused
